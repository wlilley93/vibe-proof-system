# Record 0002 — Supersession rank guard

Question: whether `supersessionLawful` should permit a lower-ranked instrument to supersede
a higher-ranked one (as first drafted, a County ruling could lawfully supersede a statute).

Disposition: no. External review caught the gap. `supersessionLawful` now takes the
superseder's kind and requires the target's rank be equal or lower; the guarantee is the
theorem `supersession_respects_rank` (Charter Art. 5 amended to state it). Existing proofs
adjusted for the strengthened existence conjunct. The genesis-digest placeholder was noted
in the same review and remains a Phase 0 obligation: the sovereign floor is only as real
as that pin.
