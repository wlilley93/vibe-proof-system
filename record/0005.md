# Record 0005 — supersession takes a list

`Instrument.supersedes` is now `List Citation` rather than `Option Citation`, so a
single enactment can retire many determinations at once. A jurisdiction that has
accumulated machinery determinations can consolidate them into one instrument
instead of filing one superseding entry per target.

The three supersession theorems are generalised rather than weakened:
supersession still never dangles, still respects rank, and entrenched law remains
immune - now for every citation in the list. The kernel builds and `vps book`
reports the lawful book unchanged; `Vps.Book`, `Vps.Examples` and `Main` compile on
the pinned toolchain.
