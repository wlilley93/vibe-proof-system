# Record 0005 — The sovereign digest becomes a parameter

Question: how can one verified kernel serve more than one jurisdiction?

Before: `genesisDigest` was a `def` inside `Genesis.lean`, so a downstream repository could
obtain its own genesis only by editing constitutional text. That made every jurisdiction a
private fork of the engine, and nothing could check that the forks still agreed — the exact
correspondence problem ASSESSMENT.md calls a watcher watching a watcher. It also blocked the
only clean fix, which is to consume the kernel as a pinned dependency rather than vendoring
a copy per repo.

Disposition: the digest is now a parameter. `genesisInstrument (d : String)`,
`authorityResolves (d : String) …`, `authorised (d : String) …`, and `Lawful (d : String) :
List Instrument → Prop`. All eight theorems plus `res_judicata` are re-established under the
new signature; a jurisdiction fixes `d` once, in its own `Book.lean`, and every theorem is
thereafter about that book under that genesis and no other.

The Charter is unchanged in substance. Force still descends from one sovereign text pinned
by digest, and self-made law is still a type error. What moved is where the digest is named:
at the point of use, rather than welded into the machinery.

Controls, per Art. 8 (`kernel/fixtures/TwoJurisdictions.lean`, all checked):
- POSITIVE — two jurisdictions with different digests are each lawful under their own genesis.
- POSITIVE — `sovereign_floor` still holds, now relative to the jurisdiction.
- NEGATIVE — an instrument claiming jurisdiction alpha's digest is **not authorised** in
  jurisdiction beta's book: `authorised beta bookB forgery = false`, by `decide`. A
  jurisdiction cannot borrow another's authority.

This is an Article 10-class change to genesis-line text and is recorded as such. It forces
re-proof of the whole kernel, which is the designed cost and was paid: `lake build` green,
no `sorry`, no `axiom`.
