# Record 0004 — Verified toolchain pin

Question: which toolchain the kernel's pin may claim, given that the only end-to-end
verification on record (kernel/PROVENANCE.md in the Foundry package, 2026-08-22) ran on
`leanprover/lean4:v4.15.0` — module-by-module build plus adversarial controls: a
rank-violating and an entrenchment-violating enactment each refused compilation; a lawful
supersession compiled and took effect.

Disposition: the pin is the verified toolchain, v4.15.0, replacing the unverified v4.33.0.
An unverified pin is a paper claim of exactly the kind Art. 8 forbids. Rule adopted (shared
with the Foundry plan §2): the pin moves only together with a full re-verification on the
new version — never alone. CI's checker step now falls back to the external lean4checker
at the pinned tag, since older toolchains do not bundle `leanchecker`.
